CPN Exam Dates and Costs

The CPN exam is available year-round, with flexibility for scheduling at either a PSI testing center or via live remote proctoring (LRP).

For the most up-to-date information regarding exam dates, application deadlines, and fees, please visit the Pediatric Nursing Certification Board website.

CPN Exam Format

Number of Questions: The CPN exam consists of 175 multiple-choice questions. Of these, 150 are scored, while the remaining 25 are unscored pretest questions, which are included to gather data for future exam development. new

Exam Response Options: Multiple-choice test.

Testing Options: The exam is available at designated test centers and can also be taken via Live Remote Proctoring (LRP), providing flexibility for candidates.

Time Allotted: Candidates have 3 hours to complete the exam.

Passing Score for CPN

Difficulty Level: The CPN exam is known to be challenging, with an annual pass rate of around 73% in recent years.

Passing Score: The Pediatric Nursing Certification Board (PNCB) uses a scaled scoring system from 200 to 800, with 400 as the passing score across all exam versions. This ensures consistency, regardless of which exam form a candidate takes.

Exam Statistics: Detailed statistics, including annual pass rates and the number of candidates tested, are available on the PNCB's official website.

Score Reporting: After completing the computer-based CPN exam, you will receive an unofficial pass/fail notification on-screen. Official results are emailed within 2-3 weeks.

CPN Exam Proctoring

The Certified Pediatric Nurse (CPN) exam is administered by the Pediatric Nursing Certification Board (PNCB) in collaboration with PSI. Candidates have the flexibility to choose between two proctoring options:

  • Live Remote Proctoring (LRP): This option allows you to take the exam from a location of your choice, such as your home or office. During the exam, a professional proctor monitors you in real-time via your computer's webcam and microphone to ensure the integrity of the testing process.
  • In-Person Testing Centers: Alternatively, you can opt to take the exam at one of PSI's designated testing centers, which are available across various locations. This traditional method involves attending a physical location to complete the exam under supervised conditions.

Both options are designed to maintain the exam's security and integrity while providing flexibility to accommodate your preferences and circumstances.

CPN Certification Renewal

CPN recertification is an annual requirement. Additional information can be found directly on the PNCB website. Recertification can be achieved through one of the following methods. Individuals will only need to choose one method below to recertify:

  • Contact Hours Only: 15 contact hours
  • Contact Hours + Practice Hours: 10 contact hours + 200 practice hours or 5 contact hours + 400 practice hours
  • Contact Hours + Pediatric Professional Learning (PPL): 5 contact hours + 1 PPL
  • Contact Hours + Academic Credit: 5 contact hours + 1 academic credit
  • Practice Hours + PPL: 200 practice hours + 1 PPL
  • PPL + Academic Credit: 1 academic credit + 1 PPL
  • Academic Credit + Practice Hours: 1 academic credit + 200 practice hours
  • Record Review Year: No additional activity required; a lower recertification fee applies.

Candidates must select their preferred recertification method when submitting the online application. For detailed information on the renewal process and requirements, visit the PNCB's official recertification page.
